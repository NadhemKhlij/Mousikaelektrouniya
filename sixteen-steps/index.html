A drum machine and a synth. Press Play, then click squares.
Starter beats

Drums

Click a square to place a hit. Click a drum's name to hear it. On a wide screen, the slider beside each row sets its level.

Synth

Octave 3

The melody grid only offers notes from the chosen key and scale, so anything you click fits together.

Play live with the mouse, touch, or your computer keys: A S D F … for white keys, W E T Y U for black keys, Z / X to change octave, Space to play or stop.
