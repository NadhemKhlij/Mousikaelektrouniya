Bendir & Raï

Tap the drum heads and play the keys. Start a rhythm underneath when you want one.

Percussion

Dum, Tak and Ka are the three darbuka strokes. On a computer: B Dum, N Tak, M Ka, or the number keys 1–8.

Rhythm

With Record on, every drum you tap while the rhythm plays is added to the pattern. Your own pattern is kept under “Mine”.

Edit the pattern step by step

Raï keyboard

Computer keys: A S D F … white keys, W E T Y U black keys, Z / X octave. Hold ← → to bend a semitone, ↓ ↑ a quarter tone. Drag the Bend strip and it springs back.
