Drums

Click a square to add a hit, or drag to paint several. Hits on an Accent step are louder. Click a drum's name to mute it.

Sound

You can also drop an audio file straight onto a pad. Your sounds stay in this browser and are not included in shared links.

Acid bass

Each step plays one note: click a square in a column to pick its note, and click it again to make the step silent. Up and Down move a note by an octave, Accent makes it louder and brighter, and Slide glides into the next note without a new attack. Turn up Resonance and move Cutoff while it plays for the acid sound.

Chords

Notes

Each row is a chord from the key (in a minor key: i, III, iv, v, VI and VII). Click a square to play that chord on that step, and click it again to remove it. Organ and Stab are short hits for house and techno; Pad holds the chord.

Effects

low-pass ← off → high-pass

Pump makes the bass and chords duck on every kick and swell back, the breathing feel of house and techno. To save a filter sweep in the pattern, press ● Record filter moves and move the Filter while it plays; the moves appear in the Filter moves row under the drums and play back every time. Click a square in that row to remove a move.
