## Next: real instrument recordings

Nadhem will bring real instruments to record. Their recordings will replace the sounds that are stand-ins or synthesized today:

| Instrument | What it uses now |
|---|---|
| Oud | A synthesized model, tuned against measurements of real oud recordings |
| Gasba | A pan-flute recording with added breath |
| Zorna | A shehnai recording |
| Shqashiq | Finger cymbals |
| Tbal (thin stick) | A small frame drum |
| Bendir (edge) | A muted small frame drum |
| Darbuka, bendir, tar | Real recordings, but not of Tunisian instruments |

### How to record

- A phone is fine. Use a recorder app that saves **WAV** if it can (otherwise the best quality it offers).
- Record in a quiet room with soft furnishings, away from walls, fridges and fans.
- Hold the phone about 30 cm from the instrument and keep it in the same place for the whole session.
- Leave about a second of silence before and after each sound, and let each one ring until it stops.
- Do not add effects, reverb or noise reduction.
- One file per sound, named after what it is, for example `darbuka-dum-hard-1.wav`.

### What to record

**Percussion.** For each stroke, record 3 hard hits and 3 soft hits.

- **Darbuka:** Dum (centre), Tak (right edge), Ka (left edge), Slap, and a 3-second roll.
- **Bendir:** Dum (centre) and Tak (edge).
- **Tar:** a hit on the skin, a short jingle shake, and a 3-second jingle roll.
- **Tbal:** a big-stick hit and a thin-stick hit.
- **Shqashiq:** a single clack from each hand, and a few seconds of the galloping pattern.

**Melody.** Record single notes, each held about 3 seconds.

- **Oud:** every open string, then notes every 3 semitones (for example C, E♭, F♯, A) from the lowest string up about two octaves. Use one normal pluck per note, and add a few tremolo notes too.
- **Gasba and zorna:** every note the instrument plays comfortably, from lowest to highest, held steady. Add a couple of notes with the player's usual vibrato and ornaments.
- **Quarter tones:** if the player uses them (for example E half-flat in Rast or Bayati), record those notes too.

Once the recordings are in hand, they get trimmed, levelled and packed into the sound packs in `bendir-rai/s/`.
